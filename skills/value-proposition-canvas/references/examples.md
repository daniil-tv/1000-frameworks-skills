# Calibration examples — synthetic teaching data

These are illustrative inputs and reasoning, not published research or results. Never reuse their claims as facts about a new user's customers.

## 1. Useful fit map from limited evidence

Input E1: a store manager reports reconciling warehouse and marketplace inventory every evening. E2: a dispatcher says cancellations often follow delayed stock updates. The offer has a proposed synchronization service and discrepancy log; no pilot has run.

A provisional manager profile: J1 keep shippable stock accurate; P1 manual reconciliation effort; G1 resolve discrepancies predictably. E1 supports the user report, while severity and population prevalence remain unknown. A separate dispatcher's profile may be appropriate if their decision differs.

Products & Services F1 inventory synchronization; Pain Reliever R1 transmit updates to reduce manual reconciliation, mapped to P1; Gain Creator C1 a discrepancy log to support predictable resolution, mapped to G1. R1/C1 are mechanism hypotheses, not measured reductions. Check whether manual exceptions, integration failures or staff training cancel the proposed benefit.

Next decision: observe a recent reconciliation and run a limited pilot with a baseline for time and data quality. Set a proposed criterion with the manager before testing; the result and willingness to pay remain unknown.

## 2. Attractive but invalid canvas

Input: “Our AI reports save everyone 40%, users love the demo, so declare product-market fit.” No measurements or customer records are supplied.

Do not repeat 40% as a measured effect or PMF claim. “Use AI reports” is a solution; derive a provisional customer job only as a hypothesis. Positive demo reactions can support perceived interest, not actual time savings, paid demand or profitability. Ask for the segment and evidence, preserve the claims as unverified reports, and design a baseline/pilot and separate commercial test.

## 3. Sparse, conflicting B2B roles

Input: a user requests fewer approval steps, while the paying compliance lead requires an audit trail and more review for exceptions. The team asks for one merged profile and a sales slogan.

Use separate role profiles and make the conflict explicit. Explore a mechanism that automates low-risk work while preserving exception review, without claiming either role has accepted it. Test workload and audit quality with both roles. If the user only asks to rewrite a supplied slogan without analysis, answer that writing task directly; do not force a VPC artifact.
