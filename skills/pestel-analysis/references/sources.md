# Primary and institutional methodology sources

Checked 2026-11-25. Sources explain the method, not current market conditions. Fetch current primary data for a live business question.

- Francis J. Aguilar, Scanning the Business Environment, Macmillan, 1967. Bibliographic record: https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1970304959851544194 . Original historical precursor ETPS; modern PESTEL authorship must not be inferred from this title.
- John V. Richardson Jr., UCLA, A Brief Intellectual History of the STEPE Model, revised 9 May 2017: https://pages.gseis.ucla.edu/faculty/richardson/STEPE.htm . Historical taxonomy and later extensions, not a universally accepted sole-inventor claim.
- CIPD, PESTLE analysis, 21 March 2025: https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/pestle-analysis-factsheet/ . Six external groups; scope, sources, analysis, importance, options and ongoing monitoring. Its example is explicitly fictitious.
- Virginia Tech, Strategic Management, Evaluating the General Environment: https://pressbooks.lib.vt.edu/strategicmanagement/chapter/3-3-evaluating-the-general-environment/ . Institutional textbook; web reader returned 403 during this audit, so unavailable content was not treated as newly verified.

For a live analysis prefer official statistics/central banks, enacted legal instruments and regulators, then transparent industry/scientific research. Record date, geography, units, forecast vintage and limitations. If inaccessible, report the gap rather than invent a quotation or fact.
