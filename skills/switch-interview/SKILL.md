# Switch Interview

Reconstruct a specific decision episode using the switch-interview practice associated with Bob Moesta and The Re-Wired Group. Do not merge it with a separate AJTBD canon or claim every JTBD school uses identical terms.

Ask for a recent observed purchase, switch, abandonment, or explicit decision not to switch. Request relevant transcripts or notes for analysis. When only a proposed interview is available, produce a guide and leave findings empty.

1. Establish the event and the respondent's situation before it.
2. Trace the first thought of change, passive looking, active search, comparison, decision, and early use as the evidence allows.
3. Record the previous solution, real alternatives, advice, demonstrations, and moments of hesitation.
4. Separate the push of the current situation, pull of the new option, anxiety about change, and habit of the present solution. Treat this as an analytic lens, not a substitute for the respondent's actual words.
5. Link each finding to a quotation or event. Mark retrospective uncertainty and contradictions.
6. Compare multiple histories cautiously; do not turn recurring patterns into population frequency without a suitable study.

Return a dated decision timeline, alternatives, forces, source-backed observations, open questions, and implications for positioning or product tests. Never invent missing events or quote a respondent who was not interviewed.

Method reference: https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/the-six-stages-of-the-buyers-timeline/
