# Method and decision boundaries

The strategy canvas is a diagnostic and action framework attributed to Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne. The horizontal axis represents the factors an industry competes on and invests in; the vertical axis is the offering level buyers receive. A value curve depicts an offering's relative profile over these factors. It records the present landscape and supports consideration of a future value configuration. The authors direct attention beyond direct rivals to alternatives and noncustomers.

## Evidence-first comparison

Frame a segment and actual choice, not a broad industry with incomparable bundles. Specify factor meanings and whether a higher value means more of the offering, more cost to the buyer, or something else. Raw price, completion time, number of choices and qualitative service statements cannot be compared by putting their raw numbers on one shared scale. Propose anchored mappings for discussion, retaining raw observations and uncertainty. If a mapping would be arbitrary, provide the evidence matrix and leave ordinal plotting pending. A label such as 1–5 is insufficient without common criteria. Offering level does not mean customer importance or satisfaction.

Use the same factor sequence for every curve. Missing is not zero, midpoint or the competitor's average. Plot known segments with gaps, or explicitly report why no defensible numeric curve is possible yet. An alternative outside the category can still be a genuine substitute; unfamiliarity alone does not exclude it. Conversely, a brand in the same category is not automatically considered by this buyer.

## Future curve

Four Actions asks what to eliminate, reduce, raise and create relative to the industry's strategic logic; ERRC records those actions. Do not redesign only by adding premium factors. Seek buyer utility while testing cost implications; the canvas itself is not an accounting model. Distinguish the as-is observation matrix from proposed to-be levels. New factors require explicit discussion of applicability and evidence for existing alternatives: never assign zero by default. A proposed level is a design target, not validated performance.

The authors' three qualitative diagnostics are focus, divergence and a compelling truthful tagline. None is a measured probability of success. A crossing pattern, curve area, high total score or Euclidean distance is not an original-method success criterion. Do not produce a fabricated weighted score or statistical significance from ordinal judgments.

## Historical and insufficient evidence

Yellow Tail is an author-documented illustration of reconfiguring US wine competition, including easier drinking/selection and fun, with Chardonnay and Shiraz at launch. Do not reconstruct exact historical competitor scores, profits or a causal impact estimate from the illustration. Preserve who reported the case and what is not independently available. If no evidence is supplied, return a labelled hypothetical factor framework, unknown matrix and targeted collection plan; not an empirical canvas. User requests for invented research remain requests, not evidence.

These operational evidence/plotting safeguards are implementation guidance for this skill; they are not additional claims made by the original authors.
