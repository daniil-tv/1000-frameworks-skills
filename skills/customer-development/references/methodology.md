# Stage gates and evidence

Original method: Steve Blank's four iterative stages. This skill's source/status register and proposed decision thresholds are operational conventions.

| Stage | Work | Evidence needed before the next commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Test problem, customer, proposed solution, business-model assumptions | Relevant behavior and solution tests; clarify remaining assumptions |
| Validation | Test a repeatable sales/acquisition or meaningful-use path | Observable commitments/use, cohort definitions, acquisition/delivery economics |
| Creation | Build demand through a demonstrated path | Evidence that acquisition remains viable beyond founder-led exceptions |
| Company Building | Organize execution | A model sufficiently established for the organizational commitment |

Failure during Validation can send the team back to Discovery. There is no universal number of interviews, conversion cutoff, or payment count that mechanically advances a company.

Use evidence suited to the claim: an interview can clarify circumstances and objections; a preorder can demonstrate a particular commitment under its conditions; repeat use tests sustained value; a representative estimate requires an appropriate design. Do not interchange these.

A test card must say what observation would change the decision. When no threshold existed before collection, label analysis exploratory; do not pretend a post-hoc threshold was predeclared. Inconclusive is a legitimate outcome. For convenience samples, show counts/denominators and recruitment limits; never generalize their rate to the market automatically.
